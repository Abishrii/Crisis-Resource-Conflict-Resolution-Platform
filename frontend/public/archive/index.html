02 / Trust & accountability

Privacy, access,
and responsibility.

This India-focused workspace stores its records on the ResQSync server. Its starting incidents are fictional sample data, and it is not an official emergency service. Applicable Indian privacy, procurement, security, and data-residency requirements must be reviewed before deployment. Cross-border requirements depend on the organization and jurisdiction.

01

Data sovereignty

Define hosting region, organization access, retention, and data flows for the intended Indian deployment. Data is stored wherever this server and its database are hosted; no residency or legal compliance commitment is implied until you have reviewed that.

02

Authentication & access

Every person signs in with their own account, and the server checks their role on every action. Sessions use an HTTP-only cookie, passwords are stored as Argon2 hashes, and every change is written to an audit trail. Connect your organization's identity provider and review organization isolation before wider use.

03

Cross-border requirements

FedRAMP, HIPAA, and CJIS may be relevant to specific US-facing deployments; they are not blanket Indian requirements. This concept has no FedRAMP authorization or claimed HIPAA / CJIS certification. Do not enter protected, patient, or criminal justice information.

04

Human authority

Recommendations support authorized decision-makers. ResQSync is not India's 112 emergency service, an official dispatch system, or a substitute for clinical or incident-command judgment. Nothing in this software dispatches responders by itself.

Privacy Policy +

Accounts, incidents, resources, messages, help requests (including names, phone numbers, addresses and photos), attached documents and the audit trail are stored on the ResQSync server and visible to the roles that need them. An administrator can reset the sample dataset where that is enabled. A deployment brief from the contact form is prepared locally and only downloaded when requested. External image, video, and Google Fonts providers receive network requests and may process IP addresses and technical metadata. This is a summary, not a finished privacy policy; final terms require legal review.

Terms of Service +

This is an illustrative concept, not an operational service or offer. It must not be used as the sole means of emergency communication or resource dispatch. Images depict Indian place context and labeled AI-generated response illustrations, not ResQSync customers. Demo incidents, organizations, scores, and allocations are fictional. Role controls are not authentication. Availability, fees, security, identity management, support, and data obligations require a signed agreement and production infrastructure before actual use.
