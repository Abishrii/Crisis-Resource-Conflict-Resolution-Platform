Overview

02 / Trust & accountability

Privacy, access,
and responsibility.

This India-focused workspace is an interactive product demo, not an operational service. Applicable Indian privacy, procurement, security, and data-residency requirements must be reviewed before deployment. Cross-border requirements depend on the organization and jurisdiction.

01

Data sovereignty

Define hosting region, organization access, retention, and data flows for the intended Indian deployment. No residency or legal compliance commitment is implied by this browser-based preview.

02

Authentication & access

Production requires a verified identity provider, server-side authorization, organization isolation, and monitored audit records. The demo role selector is only an interface preview and must not be used as a security control.

03

Cross-border requirements

FedRAMP, HIPAA, and CJIS may be relevant to specific US-facing deployments; they are not blanket Indian requirements. This concept has no FedRAMP authorization or claimed HIPAA / CJIS certification. Do not enter protected, patient, or criminal justice information.

04

Human authority

Recommendations support authorized decision-makers. ResQSync is not India's 112 emergency service, an official dispatch system, or a substitute for clinical or incident-command judgment. Nothing in this demo dispatches responders.

Privacy Policy +

Workspace changes, fictional user profiles, messages, and attachment metadata are stored in this browser's local storage when available. Document content stays only in the current session. Use the demo reset to restore the sample dataset. No form details or documents are sent to a ResQSync server. A deployment brief is downloaded locally only when requested. External image, video, and Google Fonts providers receive network requests and may process IP addresses and technical metadata. This is a preview notice, not an operational privacy policy; final terms require legal review.

Terms of Service +

This is an illustrative concept, not an operational service or offer. It must not be used as the sole means of emergency communication or resource dispatch. Images depict Indian place context and labeled AI-generated response illustrations, not ResQSync customers. Demo incidents, organizations, scores, and allocations are fictional. Role controls are not authentication. Availability, fees, security, identity management, support, and data obligations require a signed agreement and production infrastructure before actual use.
